07 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What warranty do I get?

A 6-month warranty on any repair, including compatible parts and logic board repairs. You get an invoice.

How long does a repair take?

Most repairs take 30–90 minutes. If an original Apple part has to be ordered by the phone's serial number, it takes 5–7 working days.

Do I need an appointment?

No. You can book online or by phone, or just walk in during opening hours.

Can I pay by card?

Yes: Visa, Mastercard, American Express and contactless.

What parts do you use?

“Original Dezmembrări” is a genuine part taken from a defective, dismantled phone. Original Apple parts can be ordered by serial number (5–7 working days). For batteries you can choose original Apple or compatible.

How much is the diagnosis?

The diagnosis is free.
