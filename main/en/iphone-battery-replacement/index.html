Good to know

Original Apple or compatible?

For most models you can choose between an original Apple battery and a compatible one; the price depends on the model (see the table). The compatible battery is a higher-capacity battery (“high capacity”).

Battery prices include 100 lei labour: for example, on an iPhone 13 the compatible battery costs 155 lei, plus 100 lei labour, which makes 255 lei.

A workshop example: on an iPhone 15 Pro Max, the original battery was replaced on the spot, in about 40 minutes, and the price was 560 lei. The phone is not reset, so your data stays intact. You just need iOS updated to the latest version, so that the calibration can be done (post of 22 Sep 2026).

The diagnosis is free, so you find out before the repair whether the problem really is the battery.

Questions

Questions about batteries

How long does a battery replacement take?

Most repairs take 30–90 minutes.

What warranty do I get?

A 6-month warranty on any repair, with an invoice. The warranty also covers compatible parts.

Do I need an appointment?

It is not required. You can book an appointment or just come in during opening hours.

What if the original battery is not in stock?

Original Apple parts can be ordered by the phone's serial number, in 5–7 working days.

Can I pay by card?

Yes: Visa, Mastercard, American Express and contactless.
