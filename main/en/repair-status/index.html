Repair status

You get a message from the shop’s internal system at every status change of your repair. If you want to ask something, send us on WhatsApp the name and phone number you left the device under, or the order number if you have it.

The form sends no data to us or to any other server: it only opens WhatsApp with the message written out. You decide whether to send it.
