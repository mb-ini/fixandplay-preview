Buy-back and trade‑in: sell us your phone

We buy iPhone and Samsung phones without physical defects. Fill in the brand, model, storage and condition; the message opens in WhatsApp, ready to send, and we’ll discuss the offer.

What we buy and how

  • Only iPhone and Samsung phones.
  • Only phones without physical defects: no cracked or broken screen or back, no bent frame, no water or other liquid damage.
  • The same conditions also apply to trade-ins.
  • On WhatsApp we agree the offer, what to bring and how payment works.
