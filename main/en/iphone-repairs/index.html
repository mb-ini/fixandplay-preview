Questions

Questions about iPhone repairs

How much does the diagnosis cost?

The diagnosis is free. For logic board repairs, the price is set after the diagnosis.

What warranty do I get on a logic board repair?

A 6-month warranty, with an invoice. The warranty applies to any repair, including logic board repairs.

How long does a repair take?

Most repairs take 30–90 minutes. For a logic board repair, ask by phone how long it will take in your case.

Do I need to book?

It isn't required. You can make a booking or just come in during opening hours.
