Warranty, parts and repair times

You get a 6-month warranty on any repair, with an invoice, including compatible parts and logic board repairs. Most repairs take 30–90 minutes, and the diagnosis is free.

Warranty

The 6-month warranty

Duration
6 months
What it covers
Any repair, including those with compatible parts and logic board repairs
Document
You get an invoice

For screen glass refurbishing, the 6-month warranty covers strictly the replaced glass and the related labour: the glass does not come away from the frame and no air bubbles appear between the glass and the display. Display problems that appear after the screen is fitted on the phone are not the shop’s responsibility.

Duration

How long a repair takes

Most repairs take 30–90 minutes. Screen glass refurbishing takes at least 3 days.

If an original Apple part has to be ordered by the phone’s serial number, the part arrives in 5–7 working days.

You can come with a booking or just walk in during opening hours.

Diagnosis

The diagnosis is free

The diagnosis costs nothing, as the price list in the shop also says (24 Oct 2026).

All prices, by model, are on the prices page. Prices may change; please confirm by phone.
