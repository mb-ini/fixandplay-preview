iPhone screen replacement in Sector 4, Vitan

An iPhone screen costs from 200 lei (iPhone 7 and 8) up to 2950 lei (iPhone 17 Pro Max), depending on the model and the part option: Original, salvaged; Original, ordered from Apple; or AfterMarket 120Hz. Most repairs take 30–90 minutes and you get a 6-month warranty, with an invoice.

Good to know

What the options on the list mean

Original, salvaged
A genuine part, taken from a defective, dismantled phone.

For the Original, ordered from Apple and AfterMarket 120Hz options, we explain the differences by phone or in the shop.

Original Apple parts can be ordered by the phone's serial number and arrive in 5–7 working days.

A workshop example: on an iPhone 17 Pro Max, the screen was replaced with an IC transfer (post of 25 August 2026).

Another example: on an iPhone 15 Pro Max, the screen and the back glass were replaced with original parts from stock, on the spot, in about an hour (post of 10 September 2026).

Questions

Questions about screens

How long does a screen replacement take?

Most repairs take 30–90 minutes. If an original Apple part has to be ordered by the phone's serial number, it takes 5–7 working days.

What warranty do I get?

A 6-month warranty on any repair, with an invoice. The warranty also covers compatible parts.

How much is the diagnosis?

The diagnosis is free.

My model is not in the table. What can I do?

Call +40 769 146 666 or send us the exact model on WhatsApp to find out the price.

Do I need an appointment?

It is not required. You can book an appointment or just come in during opening hours.
