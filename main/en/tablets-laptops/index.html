Questions about tablets and laptops

How much does the diagnosis cost?

The diagnosis is free.

Do you recover data from laptops or phones?

Yes. Data recovery costs 200 lei per hour, and data transfer 100 lei per hour.

What warranty do I get?

A 6-month warranty on any repair, with an invoice.

How long does a repair take?

Most repairs take 30–90 minutes. For laptops and tablets, ask by phone how long it will take in your case.
