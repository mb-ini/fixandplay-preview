iPhone and Apple Watch screen glass refurbishing

When we refurbish a screen, we replace only its front glass and keep the original display. For iPhone it costs from 400 lei (iPhone 12, 12 Pro and 13) up to 1200 lei (iPhone 17 Pro Max), and for Apple Watch between 500 and 850 lei. Refurbishing takes a minimum of 3 days and you get a 6-month warranty.

Good to know

Duration, loaner display and warranty

Duration
Refurbishing takes a minimum of 3 days.

Your phone stays with you, with a loaner display from us. We do not offer a loaner display for Apple Watch.
Warranty
6 months, strictly for the replaced part and the related labour.

Refurbishing takes a minimum of 3 days, because for us the quality of the repair matters, not necessarily speed. During this time, your phone stays with you, with a loaner display from us.

The warranty is 6 months and is given strictly for the replaced part and for the related labour. In other words, the warranty covers cases where the glass comes unstuck from the frame or air bubbles appear between the glass and the display.

Any problem with the screen (display) that appears after it has been fitted on the phone is not the shop's liability.

Refurbishing or a new screen

How it differs from a screen replacement

With refurbishing, only the front glass is replaced and the phone's original display stays.

With a screen replacement, the whole display is replaced with one of the part options on the list: Original, salvaged; Original, ordered from Apple; or AfterMarket 120Hz.

If you are not sure which option suits you, call +40 769 146 666.

Questions

Questions about glass refurbishing

How long does refurbishing take?

A minimum of 3 days.

Will I be without my phone until it is ready?

No. Your phone stays with you, with a loaner display from us. We do not offer a loaner display for Apple Watch.

What does the warranty cover?

The warranty is 6 months and strictly covers the replaced glass and the labour: the glass does not come unstuck from the frame and no air bubbles appear between the glass and the display. Problems with the display that appear after the screen has been fitted on the phone are not the shop's liability.

My model is not on the list. What can I do?

Refurbishing is done only for the models on the list. For other repairs, see all prices or call +40 769 146 666.
