Frequently asked questions

6-month warranty on any repair, with an invoice. Most repairs take 30–90 minutes. You can come with a booking or just walk in.

What warranty do I get?

A 6-month warranty on any repair, including compatible parts and logic board repairs. You get an invoice. Details: warranty and parts.

How long does a repair take?

Most repairs take 30–90 minutes. If an original Apple part has to be ordered by the phone’s serial number, it takes 5–7 working days.

Do I get an invoice?

Yes, you get an invoice.

Can I pay by card?

Yes: card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) and contactless.

Do I need an appointment?

It is not required. You can book online or by phone, or just walk in during opening hours.

What should I prepare before I bring in my phone?

We recommend backing up your data to iCloud or to Google. For an original battery replacement, update iOS to the latest version, for calibration (post of 22 Sep 2026). If you are not sure what else to prepare, call us before you come, on +40 769 146 666.

Do you speak English?

Yes, we do.

Do you buy phones (buy-back)?

Yes, but for now only iPhone and Samsung phones without physical defects (no cracked or broken screen or back, no bent frame, no water or other liquid damage). Send us the model, storage and condition on WhatsApp.

What types of parts do you use?

  • Original, salvaged: a genuine part, taken from a defective, dismantled phone.
  • Original Apple parts ordered by the phone’s serial number: they arrive in 5–7 working days.
  • For batteries you can choose between original Apple and compatible. For screens, the list has three options: “Original, salvaged” („Original dezmembrări” on the shop’s price list), “Original, ordered from Apple” („Original comandă Apple”) and “AfterMarket 120Hz”.
  • We explain the differences between the options by phone or in the shop.

How much is the diagnosis?

The diagnosis is free. See all prices.

How much does an iPhone battery replacement cost?

A compatible battery costs from 150 lei (iPhone 7 and 8), and an original Apple battery 510–670 lei (iPhone 12–17 Pro Max, Air and SE 2022), depending on the model. The price for your model.

How much does an iPhone screen cost?

A screen costs from 200 lei (iPhone 7 and 8) up to 2950 lei (iPhone 17 Pro Max), depending on the model and the part type. Prices by model.

Do you do network unlocking?

Yes, we do network unlocking. For the iPhone 16 Pro, 16 Pro Max, 17 Pro and 17 Pro Max, unlocking with Gevey eSIM costs 600 lei (posts of 21 and 25 September 2026). For other models, call +40 769 146 666.

The shop’s price list (24 Oct 2026) also includes iOS or Android software reinstallation, at 100 lei. See all prices.

How do I find out the status of my repair?

You get a message at every status change, from the shop’s internal system. For questions, send us on WhatsApp the name and phone number you left the device under, or the order number if you have it.

Are you open on Saturdays?

Yes, on Saturdays we are open from 11:00 to 15:00. From Monday to Friday our opening hours are 11:00–18:00, and on Sundays we are closed.
